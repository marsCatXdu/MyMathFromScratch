# Week 1 — Linear Algebra Foundations

## Purpose

This week diagnoses whether the basic language of linear algebra is stable enough for later work in convex optimization. The target is accurate reasoning with vectors and matrices, not speed or memorization.

## Sessions

1. [Vectors, notation, and dimensions](Day-01-vectors-and-dimensions.md)
2. [Linear combinations and span](Day-02-linear-combinations-and-span.md)
3. [Matrices as linear actions](Day-03-matrices-and-matrix-vector-products.md)
4. [Inner products, norms, and distance](Day-04-inner-products-and-norms.md)
5. [Linear equations and synthesis](Day-05-linear-equations-and-synthesis.md)
6. [Weekly review](Week-Review.md)

## Suggested pace

Allow about 45–75 minutes per session. Stop after 90 minutes even if unfinished; record where reasoning failed and resume later. Do not use a calculator for the small arithmetic exercises.

## Completion criterion

Before advancing, you should be able to:

- state the dimensions of every vector and matrix in an expression;
- interpret a matrix-vector product as a linear combination of columns;
- compute and interpret inner products, norms, and distances;
- translate a small system of equations into matrix form;
- distinguish no solution, one solution, and multiple solutions in simple cases.

The challenge exercises are diagnostic and are not required for progression.
